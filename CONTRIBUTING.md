# Contributing

Thank you for helping B.Pharm students learn Python! 💊🐍

## Easy ways to help
- Report a mistake or confusing explanation (open an **Issue**).
- Suggest a new pharmacy-based lesson or challenge.
- Fix typos, improve explanations, or translate lessons.

## Adding or editing a lesson
1. Open `assets/lessons.js` and copy an existing lesson block.
2. Give it a new `id` and fill in all fields (see the comment at the top of the file).
3. Make sure the `solution` passes your own `check`, and that the `starter` does **not** pass.
4. Open `index.html` in a browser and test the lesson.
5. Send a Pull Request, or e-mail your idea to **coregenix@rediffmail.com**.

## Rules for pharmacy content
- Use realistic but **educational** numbers.
- Never present examples as real dosing advice.
- Keep language simple: the learners are beginners.
